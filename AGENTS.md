Preserve frozen experimental records and source/protocol hashes. Keep code, derived result tables and raw experimental evidence separate. Read the English handoff and metric/cohort definitions before scientific comparisons.

Use available CPU/GPU resources efficiently when compatible with the registered recipe. Warm and precompile identical operators before round loops; avoid repeated compilation. Offline verifiers and saved-evidence audits issue no new model calls, experimental queries or GP fits. Materialize exact native sources into a separate workspace before native hash/source audits. The ordinary portable tree retains three documented standalone adaptations. Technical replay success does not establish optimization improvement.
