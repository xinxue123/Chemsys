HYPOTHESIS-CONSTRAINED NATIVE qLogEI WITH FEEDBACK-TRIGGERED REFRESH

Development study registered before experimental LLM calls, 5 November 2026.
Eight tasks; seed=0; zero free initial observations, 20 paid experiments.
Controls: immutable cold-start pure BO and grounded LLM first point then 19 native BO.
The new arm locks the latter control's exact first physical input. A new isolated
LLM, blind to its outcome, supplies hypotheses justifying that initial proposal.
This pairing tests adding hypothesis restrictions and refresh, not changing the
initial experiment. It is not a wholly independent end-to-end first-point repeat.

The LLM receives verified reaction/process background, molecular identities,
physical units and input-only examples. On refresh it receives all own measured
experiments, prior locked predictions, KF evidence, permissions and event reasons.
No current GP rankings, unqueried objectives, other trajectory, or post-hoc optimum
comparison is given. Responses are fresh isolated gpt-6.1-terra medium subagents.
Their actual assignment and raw response receipts are retained, not simulated.

At most three explicit favour/avoid scopes use actual categories and physical
bounds. At least four unqueried executable points must remain in the combined
proposed region. After the first experiment the unchanged native GP maximizes
native qLogEI in the intersection of eligible favour scopes minus avoid scopes.
No global qLogEI-gap threshold applies. The gap to unrestricted BO is logged.
If there is no eligible rule or no remaining joint candidate, fall back to native
full-pool BO and request review subject to the call schedule. Refreshed LLM
preferred prototypes are logged and mapped by input distance with categories
preserved; they do not bypass within-scope qLogEI maximization.

Separate predictive credibility from recommendation permission. A regional raw
mean/SD is locked before each in-scope actual measurement. First-point residuals
have no invented GP reference. Later KF updates estimate relative Brier skill
for improving the current incumbent versus the unchanged GP predictive forecast.
Prior variance .25, process variance .0025, measurement floor .0025, maximum
reliability .8, exactly the frozen V13 calibration implementation. Permission is
provisional/active/paused/probation. Favour requires improvement probability >=.5,
avoid <=.5, and ordinary rules require reliability >=.2. Two negative skill updates,
two >2SD misses, low credibility, or two genuinely constraint-caused nonimprovements
pause guidance. Operational search failure is not universal chemical falsification.

Equivalent finite-pool region+direction inherits evidence across new names,
forecasts and repeated withdrawals. Accurate failure forecasts alone never restore
positive guidance. After three stagnant queries an explicit LLM retest may grant
one test per semantic rule. Positive recovery requires actual incumbent improvement
and positive GP-relative Brier gain with adequate current credibility. Historical
evidence is retained even when a successful explicit retest resets failure streaks.

Initial plus at most three refresh calls, minimum gap three queries, one reserved
for query15 or later, no refresh after query18. Refresh events: paused knowledge,
no eligible guidance, exhausted/conflicting region or three-query stagnation.
At most two fresh schema attempts per logical request; no manual scientific edits.
Each choice and its predictions are durably committed before its oracle is read.
Candidate tables preserve physical inputs, native mean/SD/qLogEI and scope flags.

Numerics: frozen ColdGP, candidate pools, normalization, feature-keyed exact ties
and GP training recipe. Eight persistent CPU workers, one BLAS thread per worker;
warm native operators once per worker and Python precompile once. No runtime JIT.
SearchAUC is the mean of 20 post-query cumulative maxima, including paid query1.
Each task keeps its own original objective units. Single-seed development results
do not establish stability or chemical correctness; audit success is not efficacy.
