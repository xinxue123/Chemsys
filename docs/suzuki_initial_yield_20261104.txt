Suzuki initial-yield sensitivity study, 2026-11-04

User budget clarification: one initial observation, nineteen pure BO selections,
twenty total paid observations. Restrict only the first point. No high-yield
initial point. No LLM or chemistry knowledge controller.

Cases: Reizman Suzuki 1–4, original frozen 4,692-point pools.
Within each case use outcome tertiles, preserving ties: low <= lower tercile,
middle > lower and <= upper, high > upper. These labels are an evaluator
intervention on initial selection. The GP receives only the actual selected
conditions and measured values, without the grouping thresholds, labels,
unqueried outcomes, or eligibility restrictions after query one.

Initial arms: low only, middle only, or uniform draw from the low/middle union.
Five starting seeds 0–4. Shared PCG64 priorities over feature-sorted candidates
within each case/seed create paired draws. Choose the minimum-priority eligible
point. Thus each mixed-arm run exactly reuses either the low or middle starting
point for that seed; it is a mixture and not an independent policy.
No additional random stream is used after initialization.
Native numerical seed=0, physical-feature keyed acquisition tie priorities,
native MixedSingleTaskGP and MC512 qLogEI, finite full pool, no repeat queries.
Use the established cold-start GP adapter, including its singleton output-scale
convention. Precompile/warm operators once per persistent worker, shared locked
prefix cache, zero operator compiles inside experiment loops.

Primary SearchAUC20 = mean of all twenty post-observation running best values.
Also SearchAUC19 = mean of the nineteen post-initial running best values,
mean gain above the initial value over nineteen rounds, final best, best after
5/10/20 observations, first high-tercile hit, first 90%/95%-of-pool-max hit,
and first attainment of the run's terminal best. Targets are evaluator-only.
Report every seed and five-seed mean/spread, pair middle-minus-low within each
case, actual trajectory sequences and selected GP mean/uncertainty/acquisition.
Do not average raw objectives across cases. Small paired samples are descriptive.

The outcome-defined starting intervention is retrospective sensitivity analysis,
not a prospective method for locating a known-yield starting experiment.
No outcome optimization over possible initial points or seeds is performed.
All sixty arm/seed trajectories are retained. Distinct initial-point trajectories
may be reused exactly via GP cache and will be explicitly identified.
