V13 benefit-source decomposition: read-only, post-hoc accounting

Population: all eight frozen historical cold-start tasks, starts 0,1,2,3,
methods V13, local adapted Reasoning BO and BO; 96 complete 0+20 trajectories.
Do not substitute development variants, warm API results or selected successes.
Keep SearchAUC = mean of all 20 post-query cumulative maxima.

Primary cutoff: query 10, inherited from the previous phase analysis. Also
report cutoffs 5 and 15 as sensitivity accounting, without selecting a cutoff
by the observed result. Decompose each paired SearchAUC difference into the
weighted record increments originating before/at the cutoff and after it.
For a common objective floor b, AUC = b + sum_j (21-j)/20 * (M_j-M_(j-1)),
where M_0=b. This is exact accounting, not a causal module effect.

Re-read native states and pre-outcome commits. Verify their hashes, budgets,
candidate membership and recorded objectives against frozen evaluation tables.
For each LLM decision, evaluate the chosen point and its own-prefix GP reference
on the existing frozen table. Compare immediate incumbent improvements; this
is a conditional one-step counterfactual, never a BO continuation or a causal
estimate of the full optimization path. Query 1 has no GP and is labeled as a
prior-menu comparison. Unqueried labels are used only in this offline evaluator.

Relate record events to declared Note use, prior Note updates, menu coverage,
and same-version next-query visibility. Feedback after an outcome cannot cause
that outcome's selection. Absence of a declared forecast does not prove absence
of indirect Insight, warning or free-form memory influence.

Retain all six registered common-prefix contexts and all conditional contrasts.
The fixed-KF/no-refresh controls start only after query 10 and inherit the
prefix's knowledge and numerical state. They do not identify the contribution
of the mechanisms before query 11. Distinct LLM realizations remain unpaired.

changes to frozen policies, prompts, thresholds, seeds, pools and old releases.
