# V13 Feedback Design

V13 separates predictive skill from permission to recommend an experiment. The implementation is in code/experiment_tools/reasoning_memory_v13/ and code/experiment_tools/cold_start_v13/.

## Two independent state channels

The predictive channel tracks GP-relative Brier-score gain with a scalar Kalman filter. It stores the estimated mean and variance, probability of positive predictive skill, bounded reliability, and update count. In frozen V13, the estimate is shown as advisory context. It leaves the GP posterior and acquisition unchanged. The later programmatic KF studies use a separate candidate-level filter and explicitly deploy its weight in the ranker.

The recommendation channel records scoped directional trials, adverse observations, accurate failure forecasts, and review events. Its states determine how a structured Note may be used.

| State | Permitted use |
|---|---|
| tentative | Declare a prospective directional test |
| allowed | Cite the Note as positive support after two declared tests improve their own pre-query incumbents |
| suppressed | Use an in-scope warning or shadow forecast |
| reviewing | Run an eligible, bounded review test |
| withdrawn | Cite retained negative observations as historical warnings |
| not_applicable | Record warning evidence for an avoidance Note |

## Commit, observe, and update

Before observing the selected outcome, the adapter commits the actual selected point, the current incumbent, applicable forecasts, declared Note uses, and hashes of the prior history and state. The resulting observation updates predictive reliability and directional evidence separately. Accurate failure can strengthen a scoped warning and suppress positive use. A declared directional test can earn directional credit through actual improvement even when that forecast scores poorly. Two consecutive unsuccessful declared directional tests can suppress the direction. Improvement at a shadow-only forecast gives no positive recommendation credit.

After three queries without a new incumbent, or after a material change in the Note's focus conditions, suppressed knowledge can enter bounded review. Categorical conditions are compared by their labels. Continuous changes use a threshold supplied in advance by the caller. Review requires two new improving directional tests to restore positive use. Failed review returns the Note to suppression. Persistent stagnation grants no repeated allowance for the same event. Relevant adverse facts and consumed review allowance carry across revisions, while a new forecast version starts with a fresh numerical filter.

## Interfaces and evidence boundaries

- adapter.public_context exposes scoped knowledge and executable review candidates.
- adapter.commit_decision validates the official proposal, required forecasts, and declared uses before the outcome is read.
- adapter.apply_feedback binds the observed result to the commitment and returns the updated states.

These checks govern structured Note use. The same physical region may still be visited under a different eligible rationale. A declared test can support a direction, while isolating the contribution of a chemical variable requires a matched intervention. The selected experiment results and comparison limits are described in results/README.md.
